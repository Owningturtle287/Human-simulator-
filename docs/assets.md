# Asset credits

## Animated person

[Locomotion collection – Sam 3D Person, Animated, Innerscene](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/locomotion-collection-sam-3d-person-animated-dc23800f). CC0 dedication. Built with MakeHuman/MPFB body, rig and skin assets. This project removes unused clips and an accessory and optimizes texture and mesh sizes.

## Brain surfaces

Derived from FreeSurfer’s fsaverage subject (aseg segmentation and pial surfaces). Fischl et al., Neuron 2002 and Human Brain Mapping 1999. The input mesh was built by [Takuhiro Nishimura, Brain_Model](https://github.com/takuhironishimura/Brain_Model/tree/1e30bf3beba09a4672913991dab612b3090d0bf2) and is served here for educational and research exploration under the [FreeSurfer Software License](https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/fswiki/FreeSurferSoftwareLicense). The atlas is a standard anatomical reference. The visualization is unsuitable for diagnosis.
